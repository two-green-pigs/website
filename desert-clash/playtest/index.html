Needs Android 7.1 or newer and a 64-bit phone (almost every phone from the last eight years).

Stuck on step 1?

It says I don't have permission

That message only means Google doesn't know who you are yet. Nothing is wrong with your account.

  1. Tap the blue Sign in button at the top right of the group page.
  2. Sign in with the Google account of your phone's Play Store.
  3. The group opens: press Join group (on a narrow screen it can be at the top, next to the group's name).
  4. Come back here and go on with step 2.

Which account is mine? Open the Play Store and tap your picture at the top right: the e-mail shown there is the one to use in all three steps.

Still stuck? Ask on our Discord or write to us, and we'll add you by hand.

While you play

How you can help

  • Play a few matches: against the AI, or online with friends in the same team.
  • Report what breaks: press Bugs and feedback in the main menu or in the match menu. The game adds a screenshot and its version for you.
  • Tell us what you think: which units feel too strong, what's confusing, what you'd love to see. Use the same button, or write to us.
  • Stay a tester: keep the game installed for a couple of weeks. Google Play counts how many testers stay, and it decides when the game can launch.
  • Leave private feedback on Google Play: the game's page in the Play Store has a box for testers. Only we can see it.
  • Join the Discord: find players for online matches, report bugs and hear about updates first, on our Discord server.
  • Bring a friend: the game is made for 3v3. Send them this page.

PC

The Windows version will have its own closed beta on Steam, once the game's Steam page is up. It won't be on any other store before that. Join the Android beta now, or join our Discord to hear first when the Steam beta opens.

Good to know

Before you join

  • It's a beta: expect bugs, balance changes and the odd crash. Updates come often.
  • No account needed: the game never asks for your e-mail or your name. Read the privacy policy.
  • Your e-mail stays in the group: joining the testers' group shares your Google account's address with us, and with nobody else.
  • Leaving is easy: press Leave the program on the testing page and uninstall the game, or leave the group.
